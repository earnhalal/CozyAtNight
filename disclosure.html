Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Current status: CozyAtNight currently contains no affiliate links and earns no commission from any product mentioned on this site.

What may change

To fund the site, some future product guides may join affiliate programmes such as Amazon Associates (separate programmes operate for Amazon.com, Amazon.co.uk and Amazon.ca). If you buy through such a link, the retailer may pay us a small commission. It does not change the price you pay.

Our rules when affiliate links are added

  • Affiliate links will be identifiable, and any page containing them will say so near the top.
  • A commission never decides a ranking. Guides explain running costs, safety features and who a product is not suitable for.
  • We will not state a price as current fact on this site — prices and availability change, so product pages will direct you to check the retailer.
  • In line with Amazon's requirements, where Amazon links are used we will include the wording: “As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases.”

Advertising

If display advertising is added later, adverts will be visually separate from editorial content and will never be written to look like a guide or review.
