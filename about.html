About CozyAtNight

CozyAtNight is an independent guide site about one simple problem: winter nights are long, homes are expensive to heat, and most advice online either sells you something or skips the numbers entirely.

Who we write for

Readers in the United States, the United Kingdom and Canada — renters who cannot change their heating system, families watching an energy bill, and anyone who has ever sat on a sofa in two blankets wondering whether a heated throw is actually cheaper than turning the heating up. Where advice differs by country — tariffs, typical heating systems, product standards — we say so instead of pretending one answer fits everywhere.

How we write

  • Method first. If a guide compares costs, it shows the formula, so you can redo it with your own tariff.
  • No invented figures. Illustrative numbers are labelled as illustrative. Prices change; methods do not.
  • No fake testing. We do not claim to have lab-tested a product we have not used. Reviews are labelled as research-based where that is what they are.
  • Safety before savings. Heating products carry real risks if misused. Every heating guide includes the relevant safety basics.

How the site is funded

In future, some product guides may contain affiliate links, which can earn us a commission at no extra cost to you. At launch the site carries no affiliate links. See our Affiliate Disclosure for how that will work when it starts.
